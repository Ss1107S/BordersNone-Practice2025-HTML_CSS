    The list of steps to make Gingerbread Cookies with Cloves:
  1. In a bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients: flour, baking soda, salt, ground ginger and ground cloves (and any additional spices if used).
  2. In another bowl, cream the softened butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Add the molasses and the egg (and vanilla extract if using) to the butter-sugar mixture and mix until well combined.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredient mixture into the wet ingredients, mixing until a firm dough forms.
  5. Divide the dough into two portions (or one thick disk), wrap each portion in plastic wrap, flatten slightly, and refrigerate for 4 to 8 hours (or until well chilled).
  6. After chilling, remove one portion of dough, lightly flour a work surface and roll out the dough to about ¼-inch (≈6 mm) thickness.
  7. Cut out shapes using cookie cutters (or form desired shapes) and place them on a lined baking sheet (with parchment paper).
  8. Preheat the oven to about 350°F (≈175 °C). Bake the shaped cookies for 8-12 minutes (depending on size and thickness) until edges are firm.
  9. Remove from the oven and allow cookies to rest a few minutes on the baking sheet, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  10. Once cooled, decorate as desired (for example, with icing) or store in an airtight container.
